# 3–5 minute jury demo

1. Sign in as `analyst@cybertrace.local` and open the dashboard.
2. Explain the totals and select a clearly labelled synthetic IOC, such as `payload-delivery.test`.
3. Show the risk score and its explicit reasons.
4. Open its related indicators and explain each stored relationship.
5. Open the linked Attack DNA profile and techniques.
6. Show the evidence-only graph and associated infrastructure map.
7. Generate a report and show its retained warnings and limitations.
8. Optionally investigate a real IOC: explain that it remains UNKNOWN unless a provider supplies evidence.

State clearly that demo records are synthetic, location is associated infrastructure rather than attacker attribution, and feed absence does not prove safety.
